Kimi Delta Attention

Gated DeltaNet with the forgetting gate split per channel: each key-dimension of the state gets its own decay rate, the memory becomes a bank of timescales — and explicit positional encoding becomes unnecessary.

KDA (Kimi Delta Attention, introduced in Kimi Linear and carried into Kimi K3) is a one-symbol edit to Gated DeltaNet with outsized consequences. Gated DeltaNet's decay \(\alpha_t\) is a scalar per head per token — when the head forgets, its whole \(d_k \times d_v\) memory fades uniformly. KDA promotes the scalar to a vector, \(\alpha_t \in (0,1)^{d_k}\): every key-channel of the state decays at its own input-dependent rate.

1. The Update

One symbol apart
Gated DeltaNet — scalar decay
\[ S_t = \alpha_t \bigl(I - \beta_t k_t k_t^\top\bigr) S_{t-1} + \beta_t k_t v_t^\top \]

\(\alpha_t \in (0,1)\): the whole state fades together.

KDA — channel-wise decay
\[ S_t = \bigl(I - \beta_t k_t k_t^\top\bigr)\,\mathrm{Diag}(\alpha_t)\, S_{t-1} + \beta_t k_t v_t^\top \]

\(\alpha_t \in (0,1)^{d_k}\): each key-channel fades at its own rate.

Read the KDA transition right to left: first each row of the state (one row per key-channel) is scaled by its own \(\alpha_{t,i}\); then the delta-rule erase-and-write (derived here) corrects the association under the current key. The transition matrix is now \(A_t = (I - \beta_t k_t k_t^\top)\,\mathrm{Diag}(\alpha_t)\) — a diagonal decay composed with a (generalized) Householder reflection. In SSM terms this is a DPLR (diagonal-plus-low-rank) transition, where Gated DeltaNet had scalar-plus-low-rank, GLA had diagonal-only, and Mamba-2 a single scalar per head. Setting every component of \(\alpha_t\) equal recovers Gated DeltaNet exactly; KDA strictly generalizes it.

2. What a Vector Gate Buys — A Bank of Timescales

With a scalar gate, one head has one forgetting timescale at a time: to hold a fact for 10K tokens while flushing clause-level noise, the model must dedicate different heads to different timescales. With a channel-wise gate, a single head's state simultaneously maintains slow channels (hold the document's topic), medium channels (track the current section), and fast channels (flush every sentence) — and reassign channels on the fly, because \(\alpha_t\) is a function of the current token.

A KDA head spans the whole family at once: the α = 0.999 channel still retains 37% of its content 1,000 tokens later while the α = 0.5 channel is empty within 10. The spread of retention curves is the memory analogue of RoPE's spread of rotation frequencies — which is precisely why it can replace them (§3).

3. Decay Is a Positional Encoding

Unrolling the recurrence (ignore the delta-rule term for a moment): the contribution of token \(s\) to the state at time \(t\) is scaled per channel by \(\prod_{u=s+1}^{t} \alpha_{u,i}\) — a quantity that depends on how far back \(s\) is and on what came in between. That is a relative positional signal, computed by the layer itself:

  • Like RoPE, it is relative and per-channel, with a spectrum of rates in place of a spectrum of frequencies.
  • Unlike RoPE, it is data-dependent — distance is measured in accumulated decay, not token count, so a topic boundary (gates slam down) counts for more "distance" than 500 tokens of steady prose.

This is what licenses the boldest choice in the Kimi Linear / K3 stack: NoPE on the global attention layers. The interleaved KDA layers supply order information to the whole hybrid stack, so the MLA layers run with no rotary encoding at all — which removes MLA's awkward decoupled-RoPE workaround and means 1M-token context extension involves no wavelength rescaling: there is nothing to rescale.

4. The Constraint That Keeps the Kernel Fast

Generality has a price at training time. The chunkwise algorithm that makes delta-rule layers trainable has to multiply a chunk's worth of transition matrices together; for scalar decay these products simplify aggressively, while a fully general DPLR transition (independent diagonal and low-rank parts) needs several extra chunk-level matmuls. KDA's design threads the needle: the low-rank part is tied to the key vector (the same \(k_t\) that does the delta-rule write), which collapses enough terms that Kimi Linear's specialized chunkwise kernel runs markedly faster than the general DPLR form — a reminder that at frontier scale, expressivity proposals live or die by whether their chunked form stays matmul-shaped. The per-channel decay products are accumulated in log space for stability, exactly like Gated DeltaNet's scalar case but per channel.

5. Output Gates, Full-Rank

Like Gated DeltaNet, KDA ends with an output gate before the gated RMSNorm — a sigmoid, input-dependent gate on the readout. Kimi Linear parameterized these gates low-rank to save parameters; K3 found the expressivity worth paying for and made them full-rank (as it also did for the sigmoid output gates on its MLA layers). The same convergent story as Qwen's gated attention: cheap multiplicative gates after the token mixer are consistently worth their cost at frontier scale — they suppress outlier activations and give heads an off switch.

6. Where It's Deployed

ModelRole of KDANotes
Kimi Linear (Oct 2025) 3 of every 4 layers, hybrid with NoPE MLA The introduction: validated the 3:1 ratio and NoPE at 48B scale
Kimi K3 (Jul 2026) 69 of 93 layers (3:1 blocks, final layer always global MLA) Full-rank output gates; carries positional signal for the whole 1M-context stack

Contrast with the parallel lineage: Qwen3.5 runs Gated DeltaNet — scalar gates — in the same 3:1 hybrid layout and keeps partial RoPE on its full-attention layers. The two stacks are near-isomorphic except for exactly the two things KDA changed: gate granularity, and whether explicit positions survive.

7. Summary

Update

\(S_t = (I - \beta_t k_t k_t^\top)\mathrm{Diag}(\alpha_t) S_{t-1} + \beta_t k_t v_t^\top\)

The edit

Scalar \(\alpha_t\) → vector \(\alpha_t \in (0,1)^{d_k}\): one decay rate per key-channel.

Payoff 1

A bank of timescales in each head — slow channels hold facts while fast channels flush noise.

Payoff 2

Channel-wise decay is a learned, data-dependent relative position code — enabling NoPE global layers and rescaling-free 1M context.

The constraint

Low-rank part tied to \(k_t\) keeps the chunkwise kernel matmul-shaped — cheaper than general DPLR.

Family tree

Mamba-2 (scalar) → DeltaNet (Householder) → Gated DeltaNet (scalar × Householder) → KDA (diagonal × Householder).
