Chunkwise Linear Attention

The recurrence is the semantics; the chunked form is the implementation. How flash-linear-attention turns sequential state updates into tensor-core matmuls — the kernel algorithm that makes DeltaNet, KDA, and every hybrid stack trainable.

Every linear-attention architecture on this site — vanilla, Gated DeltaNet, KDA — is defined by a recurrence: \(S_t = A_t S_{t-1} + \beta_t k_t v_t^\top\). Run that definition literally during training and you get \(T\) sequential steps of small elementwise operations: on a GPU whose entire throughput story is tensor cores executing large matmuls, the recurrence achieves a few percent of peak. The chunkwise form is the reason these architectures exist at frontier scale at all: it re-expresses the same math — bit-for-bit the same function — as dense matmuls within fixed-size chunks plus one small state hand-off between chunks.

1. One Layer, Three Computational Forms

FormCostParallel over T?Used for
Parallel — materialize \((QK^\top \odot M^{\text{decay}})V\) \(O(T^2 d)\) yes nothing at scale — quadratic cost surrendered the whole point
Recurrent — step the state token by token \(O(T d^2)\) no — \(T\) dependent steps decode (one step per generated token is exactly what decode needs)
Chunkwise — matmuls within chunks of \(C\), recurrence between chunks \(O(T C d + T d^2)\) yes (within chunk; \(T/C\) short sequential hops) training and prefill

Decode gets the recurrent form for free — that is the architecture's selling point. The engineering problem is training: teacher-forced training processes the whole sequence at once, and the chunkwise form is what makes that tensor-core-shaped.

2. The Chunkwise Derivation (Vanilla Case)

Notation follows the linear attention page: state \(S \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k \times d_v}\), update \(S_t = S_{t-1} + k_t v_t^\top\), readout \(o_t = S_t^\top q_t\). Split the sequence into chunks of \(C\) tokens; write \(Q_{[i]}, K_{[i]}, V_{[i]} \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times d}\) for chunk \(i\)'s stacked vectors and \(S_{(i)}\) for the state after chunk \(i\).

Step A — Split each token's output into "before my chunk" + "inside my chunk"

Token \(t\) in chunk \(i\) reads \(S_t = S_{(i-1)} + \sum_{s \le t,\, s \in \text{chunk } i} k_s v_s^\top\). The first term is shared by every token in the chunk; the second involves only the chunk's own tokens.

Step B — Both terms are matmuls
\[ O_{[i]} \;=\; \underbrace{Q_{[i]}\, S_{(i-1)}}_{\text{inter-chunk: } C \times d_k \cdot d_k \times d_v} \;+\; \underbrace{\bigl(Q_{[i]} K_{[i]}^\top \odot M\bigr) V_{[i]}}_{\text{intra-chunk: causal attention on } C \text{ tokens}} \]

\(M\) is the lower-triangular causal mask within the chunk. The intra-chunk term is literally a \(C\)-token attention (no softmax); the inter-chunk term is one dense matmul against the carried state.

Step C — Advance the state once per chunk
\[ S_{(i)} \;=\; S_{(i-1)} \;+\; K_{[i]}^\top V_{[i]} \]

One \(d_k \times C \cdot C \times d_v\) matmul per chunk. The only sequential dependency left in the whole computation is this chain of \(T/C\) cheap updates — 1/64th as many sequential steps as the recurrence, with all the heavy work parallel.

Result — the cost structure

Per token: \(\;\approx 2Cd\) MACs of intra-chunk attention \(+\;4d^2\) of state read/update — constant in \(T\). Softmax attention pays \(\approx 4Td\) per token, growing without bound. Same causal semantics, opposite scaling.

softmax attention (\(\propto T\) per token) chunkwise linear (\(C{=}64\), constant)

Constant per-token training cost, from a few hundred tokens on. Attention MACs per token during training/prefill (one head, \(d = 128\)), log–log. Softmax attention's per-token cost grows with everything before it; chunkwise linear attention pays a flat \(\approx 2Cd + 4d^2\) regardless of position. Crossover ≈160 tokens; at 64K the gap is ~400×, at 1M ~6000×. This flat line — not the recurrence — is why hybrid stacks can afford to make 75% of their layers linear: at 1M-token prefill, the few softmax layers dominate total compute and the 45–69 linear layers are nearly free. (The recurrent form has slightly fewer FLOPs still — but as \(T\) sequential steps, which is why it never trains.)

3. Adding Decay Gates

A decay \(\alpha_t\) (scalar per GDN, per-channel per KDA) turns the state carry into \(S_t = \alpha_t S_{t-1} + \dots\), and the chunkwise form absorbs it with cumulative products. Within a chunk, define \(\gamma_t = \prod_{s \le t} \alpha_s\) (accumulated in log space — cumsum of \(\log \alpha\) — since a product of 64 sub-one numbers underflows fp16 otherwise). Then the same three matmuls apply with scaled operands: queries scaled by \(\gamma_t\), keys by \(\gamma_C / \gamma_t\)-style ratios, and the chunk's state carry scaled by the full-chunk decay \(\gamma_C\). The mask \(M\) becomes a decay-weighted mask. No new sequential structure appears — the gates ride along inside the matmuls, which is exactly the property an architecture must preserve to stay trainable (and which shaped KDA's parameterization).

4. Adding the Delta Rule — the WY Trick

The delta rule breaks the easy story: its transition \((I - \beta_t k_t k_t^\top)\) is not diagonal, so a chunk's combined transition is a product of \(C\) Householder-like matrices — naively, back to sequential. The rescue is a classic from numerical linear algebra (the WY representation of products of Householder transforms): the product collapses to identity-minus-low-rank,

\[ \prod_{t \in \text{chunk}} \bigl(I - \beta_t k_t k_t^\top\bigr) \;=\; I - K_{[i]}^\top W_{[i]}, \]

where the \(C \times d\) matrix \(W_{[i]}\) is obtained by solving one small \(C \times C\) lower-triangular system built from \(\beta\) and \(K_{[i]} K_{[i]}^\top\) (the "UT transform" of Yang et al., 2024). One triangular solve of size 64, then everything else — applying the chunk transition to the carried state, computing intra-chunk corrections — is matmuls again. Gated and channel-wise variants (GDN, KDA) compose this with §3's decay scalings. The takeaway worth keeping: an architecture idea in this family survives only if its chunk-level algebra collapses like this — expressivity proposals are ultimately auditioning for a matmul decomposition.

5. FLA — the Library That Ships All of This

flash-linear-attention (fla) is the de-facto home of these kernels: Triton implementations (Triton page for the programming model) of the chunkwise and recurrent forms for essentially every modern linear-attention and SSM layer — GLA, RetNet, Mamba-2, DeltaNet, Gated DeltaNet, KDA and more — exposed as paired ops: 

from fla.ops.gated_delta_rule import chunk_gated_delta_rule      # training / prefill
from fla.ops.gated_delta_rule import fused_recurrent_gated_delta_rule  # decode

o, S_final = chunk_gated_delta_rule(q, k, v, g, beta, initial_state=S0,
                                    output_final_state=True)   # chunks of 64

Hugging Face transformers integrations (Qwen3-Next's Gated DeltaNet among them) call these kernels when installed, alongside causal-conv1d for the short convolution in front of the recurrence. Model authors ship the eager recurrence as a reference; fla supplies the speed.

The fallback is silent. fla and causal-conv1d are optional dependencies: absent, frameworks quietly run the eager Python-loop recurrence — same numbers, orders of magnitude slower, no warning. The failure signature and the argument for an explicit startup assertion are documented on the Gated DeltaNet page.

6. Why C = 64

  • FLOPs: the intra-chunk term costs \(\approx 2Cd\) per token — linear in \(C\). Smaller chunks mean fewer wasted attention FLOPs.
  • Sequentiality: the state chain has \(T/C\) dependent steps; each step is a kernel-level synchronization. Larger chunks mean fewer of them.
  • Hardware quantization: the intra-chunk matmuls must fill tensor-core tiles (multiples of 16) and keep the \(C \times d\) operands plus the \(d \times d\) state resident in SRAM — the same tiling logic as FlashAttention, with the state in the role of the running softmax accumulators.

\(C = 64\) (occasionally 128) is where these meet for \(d = 128\)-ish heads on current GPUs: big enough that matmuls saturate tensor cores and the sequential chain is short, small enough that the \(O(TC)\) term stays a minority of the work. It is an engineering constant, not a modeling one — the output is identical for any \(C\).

7. Summary

The move

\(O_{[i]} = Q_{[i]} S_{(i-1)} + (Q_{[i]}K_{[i]}^\top \odot M)V_{[i]}\); \(\;S_{(i)} = S_{(i-1)} + K_{[i]}^\top V_{[i]}\).

Cost

\(\approx 2Cd + 4d^2\) per token, constant in \(T\) — vs. softmax's \(\approx 4Td\).

Gates

Decay rides along as log-space cumulative products scaling Q, K, and the state carry.

Delta rule

WY representation: a chunk of Householder transitions collapses to \(I - K^\top W\) via one 64×64 triangular solve.

The library

fla: Triton chunkwise + recurrent kernels for GLA / RetNet / Mamba-2 / GDN / KDA; silent eager fallback when missing.

Design law

A linear-attention idea survives iff its chunk algebra stays matmul-shaped — kernels vet architectures.
