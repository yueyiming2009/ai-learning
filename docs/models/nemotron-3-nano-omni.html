Nemotron 3 Nano Omni

Extend a compact hybrid language backbone with visual and speech encoders, then train the bridge and policy to reason across a shared multimodal context.

Nemotron family · Released April 28, 2026

Lineage: Nemotron 3 Nano supplies the language backbone; C-RADIO and Parakeet supply perception. Teacher-generated training data is not a claim of Qwen or GPT weight inheritance.

Modality paths · Bridge → SFT → RL · State/media accounting · Evaluation

Coverage: official card, technical account and pinned configuration, checked November 1, 2026. Omni is a missing branch of the existing family coverage, not an October release.

1. “Omni” changes the inputs, not the output into speech

The released contract accepts video, audio, images and text and produces text. It targets document intelligence, transcription, video/speech understanding and perception inside agent workflows. The card describes a 31B-class model with roughly 3B active parameters/token and a 256K context—not an inherited blanket 1M limit from every other Nemotron model.

Nano language backbone + C-RADIO + Parakeet → adapter/encoder alignment → staged multimodal SFT with longer context → multimodal RL → BF16 / FP8 / NVFP4 deployment variants.

The MoE router selects experts from token representations; the sources do not establish a hard “vision expert versus audio expert” partition. Unified multimodal inputs do not imply each modality is assigned one dedicated expert.

3. Learn the cross-modality bridge, then train the agent policy

  1. Initialize capable components. Reuse the Nano language backbone, C-RADIO visual encoder and Parakeet speech encoder. Additional teacher models help generate and filter training annotations; those are data relationships.
  2. Align adapters and encoders. The technical account reports about 127B tokens for adapter/encoder training across mixed modality combinations. The model learns to express perception in the language backbone's representational space.
  3. Expand multimodal SFT and context. The Megatron-based pipeline broadens modality coverage and progresses through roughly 16K → 49K → 262K sequence lengths. The release account separately reports around 124M curated post-training examples.
  4. Optimize outcomes through RL. NeMo RL/Gym trains across 25 environment configurations and more than 2.3M rollouts, including visual grounding, chart/document understanding, STEM, video understanding and ASR.
  5. Package for different hardware. BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 artifacts trade memory and throughput; keep their accuracy, parser and runtime settings separate.

Do not sum every data number into one training-token count: adapter tokens, curated examples, RL rollouts and a broader card inventory of approximately 717B multimodal tokens have different scopes. Similarly, a 256K deployment context is not a promise that every video/audio duration fits alongside unlimited generated reasoning.

The technical account points to public stage recipes. Without inspecting a resolved launcher, do not assume every encoder is frozen or trained in every stage. A correct adaptation records which modules update and checks text-only regressions as well as multimodal improvement.

4. Count media tokens and both kinds of state

For a single 512 × 512 crop, 16-pixel patches yield \(32^2=1024\) patches; a 2 × 2 spatial merge would yield 256 visual tokens before temporal processing. This is a simple shape example, not a universal tokens-per-frame constant. Dynamic resolution, multiple crops, video pairing and efficient-video-sampling pruning change the count.

The decoder budget is \(T_{\rm text}+T_{\rm vision}+T_{\rm audio}+T_{\rm tools}+T_{\rm generated}\). Pruning redundant video tokens lowers decoder work, but need not remove the encoder work that produced them. A sampling rate of two frames/second and a 50% token-pruning setting do not necessarily halve end-to-end latency.

\[ M_{\rm KV}=6\cdot2\cdot2\cdot128\cdot T b_k,\qquad M_{\rm SSM}=23\cdot64\cdot64\cdot128\cdot b_s. \]

At 262,144 tokens, target BF16 KV is 1.5 GiB. One BF16 recurrent state per sequence is 23 MiB, before convolution state, caches of prior states, media activations and workspaces. At four independent sequences these target-state terms approximately quadruple.

The card lists approximate artifact sizes of 62GB BF16, 33GB FP8 and 21GB NVFP4. A “minimum GPU” row is workload-dependent: the concrete recipes change context length, concurrency, cache precision, frame selection and media limits. These settings must be qualified together, not cherry-picked from different hardware examples.

5. Multimodal quality and system capacity are different outcomes

Reported card values include OCRBench v2 EN 67.04, CharXiv reasoning 63.6, MMLongBench-Doc 57.5 and OSWorld 47.4. Task setup, media preprocessing, reasoning settings and quantized checkpoint affect the interpretation.

The technical account's roughly 9.2× video and 7.4× multi-document capacity comparisons hold per-user interactivity constant and compare sustainable aggregate throughput. They are not claims of 9× single-request latency improvement or 9× better accuracy.

For an actual agent, preserve timestamps/frame ordering, verify whether audio from a video is included, and evaluate perception-to-tool-action correctness. The model produces text; a speech-generating system still needs an output speech component.
